Keyword news alerts: the professional approach to information filtering

Financial markets generate thousands of news headlines per day. The challenge is not finding news — it's finding the specific news that matters to your positions at the moment it's published. Keyword-based news alerts solve this problem by filtering the real-time news stream to surface only headlines containing terms relevant to your trading. Instead of monitoring all headlines, you monitor a curated subset.

Professional traders have used keyword filtering on Bloomberg terminals for decades. The capability is now accessible to individual traders through next-generation news terminals — transforming reactive news consumption into proactive, targeted intelligence.

How to build an effective keyword alert system

The quality of your keyword alerts determines the quality of your news edge. A poorly designed system generates alert fatigue (too many irrelevant notifications) or missed signals (too narrow keywords). Best practices:

  • Tier 1 — Position-specific keywords: For each open position, create alerts for the specific company, currency pair, commodity or index. E.g., if long Apple stock, alert on "AAPL", "Apple earnings", "iPhone", "Tim Cook". If long EUR/USD, alert on "ECB", "Lagarde", "eurozone inflation", "European PMI".
  • Tier 2 — Macro theme keywords: Alert on the macro drivers relevant to your strategy. If you're a rate trader, alert on "Federal Reserve", "FOMC", "rate decision", "dot plot", "Powell", "inflation". If you trade commodities, alert on "OPEC", "EIA inventory", "crude oil", "natural gas storage".
  • Tier 3 — Risk monitoring keywords: Alert on systemic risk signals relevant to all positions: "banking crisis", "credit default", "central bank emergency", "geopolitical", "sanctions". These are low-frequency but high-impact — you want to know immediately.

Keyword categories for different trading instruments

Starting keyword sets by instrument type:

  • Forex (EUR/USD): ECB, Lagarde, eurozone, European inflation, German PMI, Federal Reserve, Powell, FOMC, NFP, CPI, US dollar, DXY
  • Gold (XAU/USD): Federal Reserve, real rates, TIPS yield, geopolitical, safe haven, gold demand, central bank reserves, dollar strength
  • Crude oil: OPEC, production cut, EIA inventory, Russia oil, Strait of Hormuz, API crude, rig count, energy demand
  • S&P 500 / US equities: FOMC, earnings, S&P 500, Nasdaq, recession, employment, consumer confidence, retail sales
  • Bitcoin / Crypto: Bitcoin, BTC, crypto, SEC, ETF, Binance, Coinbase, blockchain regulation, CBDC

Alert timing and delivery: avoiding alert fatigue

The most common failure mode of keyword alert systems is alert fatigue — so many notifications that traders start ignoring them. Prevention strategies:

  • Separate HIGH-impact alerts (delivered immediately via Telegram/SMS) from standard alerts (summarised every hour)
  • Review and refine keyword lists weekly — remove keywords generating irrelevant alerts, add emerging themes
  • Use phrase matching ("rate cut announcement") rather than single words ("cut") to improve precision
  • Set time-based filtering during non-trading hours to avoid overnight noise accumulation
