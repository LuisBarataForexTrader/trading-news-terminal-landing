A sale is credited to you only when the customer reaches Trading News Terminal through your referral link. Attribution is recorded in the visitor's browser when they arrive via your link and is carried through sign-up and checkout, where it is credited on the first successful payment.

The Program uses last-referral attribution: if a visitor later arrives through a different affiliate's link, the most recent link is credited. Attribution may be lost or reassigned if the visitor clears their browser storage, switches device or browser, blocks tracking, or arrives through another affiliate. We do not guarantee that every sale you influence will be attributed.

Self-referrals are not eligible. You may not earn commission on your own purchases or on purchases made using your own details.

Commissions are recorded as pending when a payment succeeds and become payable only after they have cleared the holding period in Section 6. 06 Payments & Payouts

Held and available balances

Every commission is first recorded as held. A held commission is not yet payable and is shown separately in your affiliate dashboard while the customer's refund, cancellation, and withdrawal period is still open.

A commission becomes available (valid for payout) only after a clearance period of 30 days from the date of the underlying customer payment, provided that period has passed without a refund, cancellation, or chargeback. Until then it stays held and cannot be withdrawn. The figure shown as your available balance is therefore only the amount that has already cleared this period; the remainder is displayed as held.

We may not pay a commission if the customer is refunded

If a customer is refunded, cancels under a right of withdrawal, or charges back their payment, we may withhold and cancel the related commission, whether or not the clearance period has ended. Only commissions on payments that have cleared this period and remain fully paid are treated as valid and payable to you.

Minimum balance & requests

You may request a payout once your available balance reaches a minimum of €50. Held amounts do not count toward this minimum and cannot be requested. Payouts are made in EUR by bank transfer to the details you provide, normally within 15 business days of an approved request.

Refunds & chargebacks after payout

If a payment that generated a commission already paid to you is later refunded, reversed, cancelled, or charged back (which can occur after the clearance period), the corresponding commission is cancelled, and we may offset it against your future commissions or request its return.
