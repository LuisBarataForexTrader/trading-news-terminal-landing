Why a workspace beats a single news feed

A standard news feed is one scrolling list. During a busy session you are constantly scrolling, re-filtering and losing context. A workspace puts several filtered views on screen at once, so the news that matters to your book is always visible without a single click. You stop hunting for headlines and start reacting to them.

Build a desk for each market

Traders rarely watch everything at the same intensity. With saved workspaces you keep a dedicated desk per market and load the right one in a click. A Markets Overview for the open, an FX Desk with forex news and central bank headlines next to EUR/USD, a Crypto Desk with Bitcoin and Ethereum columns, a Commodities desk with gold and oil, and a Macro desk with equities and politics next to the Nasdaq. Each desk remembers its own columns, charts and sizes.

Filtered columns: see only what moves your book

Every column is independent. Filter by asset (gold, oil, Bitcoin, a single ticker), by topic (central banks, energy, politics), by impact (high impact only), by source, or pin your watchlist. A forex trader does not need Chinese PMI unless they trade AUD, so they simply do not add that column. The result is a feed with zero noise.

Charts and news in one grid

A workspace mixes news columns and live TradingView charts in the same grid. When a headline breaks you see the candle form beside it, no tab switching. You can change a chart symbol inline, without relaunching the column, so one chart panel can follow the asset you are reading about.

Save, switch and lock

Arrange widgets by dragging and resizing, stack them, and let the layout fill the screen. Save it with a name, and it appears in your workspace menu for one click loading. Lock it when it is set so nothing moves while you trade. Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What is a workspace in Trading News Terminal?

A workspace is a custom layout of the news feed. Instead of one scrolling list, you build columns, each filtered the way you want (by asset, topic, impact, source or your watchlist), and place live charts beside them. You save a workspace for every market you follow and switch between them in one click.

Is the workspace free?

The custom workspace is part of the Pro plan. The Basic plan is free forever and includes the live news feed, economic calendar and TradingView charts. Pro adds the multi-column workspace, zero delay news, squawk audio and live TV.

Can I create a different workspace for each market?

Yes. You can save as many workspaces as you want, for example a Markets Overview, an FX Desk, a Crypto Desk, a Commodities desk and a Macro desk. Each one keeps its own columns, charts and layout, and loads in one click.

Can I put charts and news side by side?

Yes. A workspace mixes news columns and live TradingView charts freely. You can change a chart symbol inline without relaunching the column, and arrange everything by dragging and resizing the widgets.

How is a workspace different from a normal news feed?

A normal feed is a single list you scroll. A workspace is a grid you control: several filtered columns at once, charts beside the news, saved layouts per market, and a locked view once it is set. It turns passive reading into a focused trading cockpit.
