Earnings Call Transcripts, Free Live Coverage

Earnings calls drive single-stock price action more than any other event. Beats and misses on revenue/EPS matter, but the Q&A frequently moves stocks more than the headline. We surface the moments that move the tape.

Earnings calendar integration

Our calendar shows every S&P 500 company's expected report date, time (BMO/AMC), consensus EPS, consensus revenue and prior period actual. As the report drops, we update with actual values within seconds.

For high-priority reports (Nvidia, Apple, Microsoft, Tesla, JPMorgan), we provide live Q&A coverage with quote extraction every 60 seconds during the call.

Why Q&A matters more than the print

The earnings print is in the press release, anyone can parse it. The Q&A is where:

  • Guidance gets clarified or pulled
  • Analyst tone is set for the next quarter
  • Strategic shifts get telegraphed (M&A, restructuring, leadership)
  • Competitor commentary leaks
  • One offhand CFO comment moves 10% (cf. Nvidia 2024 H100 supply constraint comments)
