What Moves It

What moves AAPL (Apple)

Four families of catalysts. Knowing which one is in play avoids thesis mistakes.

📱 iPhone cycle

iPhone is ~50% of revenue. New model cycle (September) is the single biggest annual catalyst.

💼 Services revenue

Highest-margin segment growing 15-20% YoY. Multiple expansion lives here.

🇨🇳 China demand

Greater China is ~17% of revenue. Geopolitical and demand cycles move the stock.

💰 Capital returns

Buyback program is one of the largest globally. Dividend hikes annually.
