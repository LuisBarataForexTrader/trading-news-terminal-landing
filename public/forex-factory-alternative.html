A Forex Factory Alternative Built for Real-Time News

Forex Factory is a trusted free calendar and forum, and it is not going anywhere. Trading News Terminal is the upgrade for traders who need more than a schedule: a live, multi-asset news wire with a hands-free squawk and impact tagging on every headline, surfaced in seconds.

THE SHORT ANSWER

What is the best Forex Factory alternative?

Forex Factory earned its place as one of the most visited free resources in retail trading. Its economic calendar is clean and reliable, its forecasts are easy to read, and its forum holds decades of community knowledge. If all you want is a schedule of upcoming releases, it does that job well and asks nothing in return.

The honest limitation is scope. Forex Factory is built around forex and around a calendar. It does not carry a real-time news wire, it has no audio squawk, and it does not stream headlines across stocks, indices, commodities, crypto and bonds. Trading News Terminal (TNT) is built for the trader who outgrew the calendar. It keeps a live economic calendar and layers on a real-time multi-asset news feed, hands-free audio, and automatic impact tagging on every headline, so the calendar becomes the starting point rather than the whole product.

CREDIT WHERE DUE

What Forex Factory gets right

A fair comparison starts with respect, so let us be clear about what Forex Factory does well. It is a dependable free calendar with a loyal following, and for a lot of traders it is the first bookmark they ever saved.

  • A solid, free economic calendar with event times, consensus forecasts and historical values laid out cleanly.
  • An active community forum where traders share journals, systems and candid broker feedback.
  • A clear forex-first focus that suits traders who live and breathe the major pairs.
  • Zero cost, which is genuinely hard to argue with for a reference tool.
THE UPGRADE

Where Trading News Terminal goes further

The gap shows the instant the market actually moves. A scheduled event is only half the story. The other half is the headline that crosses the wire, the revision nobody forecast, or the central banker who says a little more than the market expected. A calendar cannot capture that, because by the time an event is over the move has already happened.

This is where TNT changes the game. On Pro, breaking stories surface in seconds, the instant they cross the wire, drawn from professional newswires and institutional sources. Every headline is automatically tagged to the assets it affects and classified by impact as HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW, so you can collapse the noise to only what touches your book. You can read exactly how headlines are sourced, tagged and scored in our methodology.

  • A live news feed across 6 asset classes: forex (50+ pairs), indices (S&P 500, Nasdaq, DAX, FTSE, Nikkei, VIX), stocks, commodities (energy, metals, agriculture), crypto and bonds.
  • A hands-free Squawk Box that reads breaking headlines aloud, so you never miss a print while your eyes are on the chart (Pro).
  • A live economic calendar with countdown timers, previous value and consensus forecast, and the actual figure auto-injected into the feed with a colour-coded beat or miss signal.
  • Telegram and keyword alerts that reach you anywhere, so the trade finds you instead of the other way around.
  • A full terminal around the feed: Central Banks Tracker, Market Screener, Market Sentiment, a Macro and Bonds Hub, 7 live financial TV channels, TradingView charts, and an installable PWA for your desktop or phone.
