FAQ

Is Trading News Terminal regulated by the FCA?

Trading News Terminal is a news and analytics platform, not a financial-advice or order-execution service, so it does not require FCA authorisation. We do not handle client funds, provide investment advice or execute orders.

How fast is the BoE rate decision alert?

Pro subscribers receive the BoE MPC decision within ~500 milliseconds of publication on bankofengland.co.uk. The vote split and minutes summary follow within 5–15 seconds via our parser.

Does TNT cover the LSE Regulatory News Service?

Yes. We track LSE RNS announcements for the FTSE 350 (covers all FTSE 100 and FTSE 250) including profit warnings, board changes, M&A activity, capital raises and dividend changes. AIM coverage is selective based on impact tagging.

Can I trade UK shares through Trading News Terminal?

No — TNT is news only. We provide the data that informs your trading decisions, but execution happens through your broker. Pair us with a UK-regulated broker (IG, CMC, Hargreaves Lansdown, Interactive Brokers UK).
