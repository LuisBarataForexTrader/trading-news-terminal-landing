The four events that move global markets most, with their official 2026 and 2027 dates and release times. Sourced from the Federal Reserve, the ECB and the US Bureau of Labor Statistics. Bookmark this page, then watch them live in the terminal.

If you trade forex, indices, bonds or commodities, these are the dates that reshape the tape: two central-bank rate decisions and the two US data prints that set the tone between them. Below are the confirmed schedules. Central-bank dates for 2027 are tentative until each central bank confirms them, and data release dates can be rescheduled, so always check the live calendar on the day. Times are given in the market's own time zone.

Federal Reserve

FOMC meeting dates

The Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) meets eight times a year. The rate decision and statement land at 2:00 PM ET on the second day, with the Chair's press conference at 2:30 PM ET. Four meetings a year (March, June, September, December) also publish the Summary of Economic Projections and the dot plot, which tend to be the higher-volatility events.

FAQ

Common questions

How many times a year does the Fed meet?

The FOMC holds eight scheduled meetings per year. The rate decision is announced at 2:00 PM ET on the second day, with a press conference at 2:30 PM ET, and four of those meetings also publish the Summary of Economic Projections and dot plot.

What time is NFP released?

Nonfarm Payrolls, part of the Employment Situation report, is released at 8:30 AM ET, usually on the first Friday of the month.

Are the 2027 central-bank dates final?

No. The Federal Reserve and the ECB publish 2027 dates as tentative or indicative and confirm them closer to the time. The US data release dates from the BLS are scheduled firmly but can still be revised, so check the live calendar on the day.
