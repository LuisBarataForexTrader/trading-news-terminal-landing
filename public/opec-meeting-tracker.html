Why OPEC matters for traders

OPEC+ controls roughly 40% of global crude production. A surprise cut of 500k–1M barrels per day moves Brent by $3–$6 within 24 hours. A surprise increase moves it the same magnitude on the downside.

FAQ

When is the next OPEC meeting?

Full ministerial meetings are typically held in June and December, with JMMC reviews quarterly. Our tracker pulls the dates directly from opec.org press releases and alerts subscribers 72h, 24h and 1h before each meeting.

What is OPEC+?

OPEC+ refers to the 13 OPEC member countries plus 10 non-OPEC oil producers (Russia, Mexico, Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, Brunei, Malaysia, Oman, South Sudan and Sudan) coordinating production targets under the 'Declaration of Cooperation' framework signed in 2016.

How does an OPEC decision move oil prices?

A surprise production cut typically lifts Brent crude by $2–$5 per barrel within 24 hours; a surprise increase or quota hike moves it down the same magnitude. Sustained policy shifts (multi-month cuts) can move prices $10–$15 over weeks.
