The Other Options

Other Bloomberg alternatives, briefly

Benzinga Pro — a fast, trader-focused newsfeed with an audio squawk and scanners. Strong on US equities and earnings; priced well above TNT and US-stock-centric.

Koyfin — the most-cited all-round data alternative: dashboards, screeners, fundamentals and macro, with a free tier. Great for research and data, lighter on real-time breaking-news speed.

LSEG Workspace (Refinitiv) — the closest true institutional replacement (Reuters news, deep data, Excel/API), but priced in the thousands per year, not a "cheap" alternative.

Where TNT fits: if your priority is speed and signal on market-moving news across forex, indices, commodities, crypto and macro — not full institutional data — TNT covers that layer for free or €40/month, with a squawk box and multi-asset coverage that newsfeeds built only for US stocks don't match.
